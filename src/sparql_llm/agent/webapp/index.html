Using SPARQL-LLM on the ELIXIR IMPACT project
Beta

SPARQL-LLM generates SPARQL queries from natural language questions, executes them against the relevant endpoints and summarizes the results.

In this use case, it is applied to IDSM, the Integrated Database of Small Molecules. IDSM brings together the chemistry and bioactivity sources inside the circle, and its federated queries reach the endpoints around it.

IDSM is one of the resources of ELIXIR IMPACT, a project of ELIXIR Czech Republic (IOCB Prague) and ELIXIR Switzerland (SIB Swiss Institute of Bioinformatics) that combines molecular, metabolic and clinical data with AI.
